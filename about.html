Our Story

Redefining location-based gaming environments.

Step Beyond Reality

Founded in 2024, VR Nexus Arena has quickly become a leading brand in location-based virtual reality entertainment. We combine wireless multiplayer tracking arrays with advanced physical haptics to design an unmatched immersive gaming ecosystem.

From corporate team building games to elite regional esports tournaments, we build portals that let players touch, feel, and conquer digital realities safely.

Sanitization & Safety Protocols

Immersion is nothing without safety. We maintain medical-grade sanitization practices to guarantee a clean and comfortable experience.

UV-C Light Sanitization

Every headset, controller, and haptic vest is placed inside specialized UV-C cabinets between game sessions.

Zero Motion Sickness

We calibrate all displays at a minimum of 90Hz frame rates to match sensory feedback and eliminate motion sickness.

Eco-friendly Liners

Disposable leatherette face pads are provided to every player for absolute hygiene control.
